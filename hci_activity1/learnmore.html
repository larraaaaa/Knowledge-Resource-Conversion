What is KRC?

KRC stands for Knowledge Resource Conversion, a cutting-edge web application designed to provide a simple and convenient way to convert units of measurement. Whether you're a student tackling complex equations, a professional working with international specifications, or just someone who needs quick conversions for everyday tasks, KRC is your reliable digital companion.

What We Offer

KRC offers a comprehensive range of conversions across multiple categories:

  • 📐 Area Conversions
    Convert square meter, acres, hectares, and more
  • 🏋️‍♀️ BMI Calculator
    Calculate Body Mass Index and health indicators
  • ⚡ Energy Conversion
    Convert joules, calories, BTU, and kilowatt-hours
  • 📏 Length Conversion
    Convert between meters, feet, inches, kilometers, and more
  • ⛽ Fuel Economy
    Convert MPG, L/100km, and other fuel efficiency units
